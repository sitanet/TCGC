## Slide 2: Introduction & Problem Statement
- **Challenge:** Churches need modern digital solutions to engage members
- **Key Problems Addressed:**
  - Limited access to sermons and spiritual content outside church
  - Inconvenient traditional giving methods
  - Difficulty staying connected with church events and community
  - No centralized platform for church activities

---

## Slide 3: App Overview
- **What is TCGC?**
  - A Flutter-based cross-platform mobile application
  - Available on iOS, Android, Web, Windows, macOS, and Linux
  - Designed specifically for The CityGate Church congregation
- **Core Purpose:**
  - Digital spiritual companion
  - Seamless giving platform
  - Community engagement hub

---

## Slide 4: Key Features Overview
- **Audio Sermons & Messages** - Listen to teachings anytime, anywhere
- **Video Content** - Watch church videos and livestreams
- **Digital Wallet (TCGC Wallet)** - Secure giving platform
- **Events Calendar** - Stay updated on upcoming church events
- **Teens Content** - Dedicated section for youth
- **Profile & Notifications** - Personalized experience

## Slide 8: Wallet Security & Features
- **Security Features:**
  - NIN/BVN verification for identity
  - Transaction PIN protection
  - Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face)
  - Bank-grade encryption
- **Powered By:**
  - 9 Payment Service Bank (9PSB) - CBN licensed
  - Sitanet Global Orbit (Technology Partner)
- **Additional Features:**
  - Transaction history
  - Fund wallet easily
  - Send money functionality

## Slide 14: Technical Architecture
- **Built With:**
  - Flutter (Dart) - Cross-platform framework
  - Riverpod & Provider - State management
  - Firebase - Push notifications & messaging
  - SQLite - Local data storage
  - Just Audio - Audio playback engine
- **Platforms Supported:**
  - iOS, Android, Web, Windows, macOS, Linux
