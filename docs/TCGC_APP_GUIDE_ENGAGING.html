Creating Your Account

🆕 First Time? Here's How to Sign Up

1
Open the App & Tap "Sign Up"

You'll see a beautiful welcome screen. Choose the Sign Up option.

2
Fill in Your Details

Enter your name, email address, and create a password you'll remember.

3
Check Your Email 📧

We'll send you a confirmation link. Click it to activate your account!

4
Sign In & You're Ready!

Come back to the app, sign in, and start exploring. Welcome home! 🎉

💡

Pro Tip!

Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder first. Still nothing? Tap "Resend Confirmation" and we'll send another one.

Already Have an Account?

Even easier! Just tap "Sign In," enter your email and password, and you're in! 🎯

👆

Use Your Fingerprint!

Hate typing passwords? After your first login, you can enable fingerprint or face recognition. One touch and you're in! Find this in Profile → Settings → Biometric Login.

Forgot Your Password? 🤔

It happens to the best of us! Here's how to get back in:

  • Tap "Forgot Password" on the sign-in screen
  • Enter your email address
  • Check your email for a reset link
  • Create a new password
  • Sign in with your new password - easy!

Giving Made Easy 💝

💰

No more forgetting your offering at home. No more looking for your wallet during service. Give tithes and offerings right from your phone - anytime, anywhere!

Setting Up Your Wallet (One Time Only!)

Before your first giving, you'll set up your wallet. It takes just a minute:

💳 Quick Wallet Setup

1
Go to Wallet & Create

Tap the 💰 icon, then "Create Wallet"

2
Get Your Account Number

You'll instantly receive a personal bank account number just for you!

3
Create Your PIN

Choose a 4-digit PIN to protect your transactions. Keep it secret! 🤫

4
Verify Your Identity (Optional)

Add your NIN to unlock higher giving limits. But it's optional - basic limits work for most people!

Adding Money to Your Wallet

Think of your wallet like a digital purse for church giving. Here's how to fill it up:

💵 How to Add Money

1
Tap "Fund Wallet" or "Add Money"

You'll see your personal account number and bank name

2
Copy the Account Number

Tap to copy it to your clipboard

3
Transfer from Your Bank

Open your bank app (any bank works!) and transfer to that account

4
Money Appears Automatically!

Usually within seconds, your wallet balance updates. Magic! ✨

🏦

Works with Any Bank!

GTBank, Access, First Bank, Zenith, UBA - literally any bank in Nigeria. Just do a normal transfer to your wallet account number!
