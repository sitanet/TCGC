Welcome to TCGC Mobile App

TCGC Mobile App brings the church to your fingertips. Listen to sermons, watch live services, give offerings, and stay connected with your church family - all from your phone.

What You Can Do With This App

  • Listen to Sermons - Access all church messages anytime
  • Watch Live Services - Join services from anywhere in the world
  • Give Offerings & Tithes - Convenient and secure digital giving
  • Download for Offline - Listen without internet connection
  • Stay Updated - Get notifications about church events
  • Teen Content - Special section for young members

Getting Started

Creating Your Account

When you open the app for the first time, you'll see the welcome screen with two options:

How to Sign Up (New Members)

1
Tap "Sign Up" on the welcome screen
2
Enter your details: First name, Last name, Email address, and create a Password
3
Check your email for a confirmation link
4
Click the link in the email to activate your account
5
Return to the app and sign in with your email and password

💡 Tip

If you don't receive the confirmation email, check your spam folder. You can also tap "Resend Confirmation" to get a new email.

Signing In (Existing Members)

How to Sign In

1
Tap "Sign In" on the welcome screen
2
Enter your email and password
3
Tap "Sign In" button

🔐 Fingerprint/Face Login

After your first login, you can enable fingerprint or face recognition for faster access. Go to Profile → Settings → Biometric Login to turn this on.

Forgot Your Password?

Don't worry! You can easily reset it:

  1. Tap "Forgot Password" on the sign in screen
  2. Enter your email address
  3. Check your email for a reset link
  4. Click the link and create a new password
  5. Sign in with your new password

Choosing Your Interests

After signing in for the first time, you'll be asked to select topics you're interested in. This helps us show you content you'll love!

Available Topics

  • Sunday Service Messages
  • Bible Study
  • Prayer & Worship
  • Family & Marriage
  • Youth & Young Adults
  • Leadership
  • Special Programs

Why Choose Interests?

When you select your interests, the app shows you messages and content that match what you care about. You can change these anytime in your profile settings.

Playing a Message

When you tap on a message, you'll see the full player with these controls:

Player Controls

  • Play/Pause Button - Start or stop the message
  • Skip Forward - Jump ahead 15 seconds
  • Skip Backward - Go back 15 seconds (great for replaying important points!)
  • Progress Bar - See how far you are; drag to any point
  • Time Display - Shows current time and total length

🎧 Listen While Doing Other Things

You can keep listening even when you:

  • Leave the app
  • Lock your phone
  • Use other apps

The audio keeps playing! You'll see playback controls on your lock screen and in notifications.

Mini Player

When you're listening and move to other parts of the app, a small player appears at the bottom showing what's playing. Tap it to go back to the full player.

Special Features

⬇️ Download for Offline

Tap the download button to save a message to your phone. Listen anytime without internet - perfect for:

  • Traveling on planes
  • Areas with poor signal
  • Saving mobile data

❤️ Favorites

Tap the heart icon to save messages you love. Find all your favorites in one place for easy access later.

📤 Share

Tap the share button to send a message link to friends and family via WhatsApp, text, or any app.

📋 Playlists

Create your own collections of messages. Great for organizing sermons on specific topics you want to revisit.

📍 Resume Where You Left Off

The app remembers exactly where you stopped listening. When you come back, it picks up right where you left off - even after closing the app!

Giving & Wallet

The Wallet feature makes giving tithes and offerings simple, secure, and convenient. Give anytime, anywhere - not just on Sundays!

Setting Up Your Wallet

Before you can give, you need to set up your wallet. Here's how:

One-Time Wallet Setup

1
Go to Wallet tab and tap "Create Wallet"
2
You'll receive a bank account number - this is YOUR personal account for church giving
3
Create a 4-digit PIN - you'll use this to approve transactions
4
Verify your identity (optional) - enter your NIN to unlock higher giving limits

Adding Money to Your Wallet

How to Fund Your Wallet

1
Tap "Fund Wallet" or "Add Money"
2
You'll see your account number and bank name
3
Copy the account number
4
Open your bank app and transfer any amount to that account
5
The money will appear in your wallet automatically!

💡 Good to Know

You can transfer from any bank in Nigeria - Access, GTBank, First Bank, Zenith, or any other. The money usually arrives within seconds!

Giving Tithes & Offerings

How to Give

1
Tap "Give" in your Wallet
2
Select type - Tithe, Offering, Thanksgiving, Seeds, etc.
3
Enter amount (minimum ₦100)
4
Review and tap "Continue"
5
Enter your 4-digit PIN
6
Done! You'll see a confirmation with receipt details

Giving Options Available

TypeDescription
TitheYour 10% giving
OfferingGeneral church support
ThanksgivingSpecial gratitude offering
SeedsFaith seed giving
Building FundChurch building projects
MissionsSupport missionaries and outreach

Wallet Levels

Your wallet has different levels based on verification. Higher levels allow larger transactions:

LevelDaily LimitHow to Get
Basic₦50,000/dayVerify your NIN
Standard₦500,000/dayAdd BVN verification
Premium₦5,000,000/dayComplete full verification

💡 For Most People

The Basic level (₦50,000/day) is enough for regular tithing and offerings. You only need to upgrade if you want to give larger amounts.

Viewing Your Giving History

All your transactions are saved. You can:

  • See every tithe and offering you've given
  • View transaction dates and amounts
  • Get reference numbers for your records
  • Filter by date or type

Benefits of Digital Giving

  • Give anytime - Not just on Sundays
  • Never forget - Set up your giving when you remember
  • Complete records - Track all your giving in one place
  • Secure - Protected by your PIN
  • Private - No handling cash in public

Tips for the Best Experience

📥 Download Sermons on WiFi

Save your mobile data! Download sermons when you're connected to WiFi, then listen anytime without using data.

🔔 Enable Notifications

Turn on notifications to never miss a new sermon or live service. You can always customize which notifications you receive.

❤️ Use Favorites

Tap the heart on messages you love. Build your personal collection of favorite sermons for easy access.

🔐 Keep Your PIN Safe

Your transaction PIN protects your wallet. Don't share it with anyone and choose a number that's not easy to guess.

📱 Keep the App Updated

When you see an update available, install it to get the latest features and improvements.

🌙 Try Dark Mode

If you often use the app at night, try dark mode - it's easier on your eyes!
